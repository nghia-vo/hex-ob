Everything up to TTLOUT1 is exact — every pulse is tracked and its angle captured. The TTL cable is replaced by a bridge on the tally (verified: 5 pulses → 5 pokes → 5 real frames): watching the cumulative counter instead of the pulse bit means a missed update heals by the delta, and the counter's reset-on-arm rebaselines it. Opt-in: the free-running pyepics oracle must not run it (double-drive) — its totals-based checks (361 = 361 = 361) never needed per-pulse correspondence.

The one-sentence answer

Block-to-block signals are exact — the sim runs the beamline's real Tomo_radio_1_config design on the cycle-accurate PandABlocks-FPGA block-sim engine. Box-to-box wires are software bridges, both verified — motor→encoder always on; PandA→camera TTL opt-in (one frame per pulse; keep it off for free-running runs like the pyepics oracle).
